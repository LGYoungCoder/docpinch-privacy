Privacy, made readable

Your documents stay with you.

DocPinch turns selected photos into PDF files on your Android device. We do not operate a document-conversion server, and your photos and generated PDFs are not uploaded to us.

Effective: August 30, 2026  ·  Last updated: August 30, 2026

On-device processing

Selected photos, page edits, compression, and PDF creation stay on your device.

Ads stay separate

Ads may use device and interaction data, but DocPinch never gives the advertising SDK your photos or PDFs.

Minimal access

Android system pickers give DocPinch access only to photos and save locations you choose.

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how the Android application DocPinch: Image to PDF Maker ("DocPinch", "the app", "we", or "us") handles information. It applies to the app and this public privacy-policy website.

DocPinch is designed as a local-first utility. You do not need to create a DocPinch account to select photos, edit pages, create a PDF, save it, or share it.

3. Local processing and temporary files

Image decoding, crop and rotation, compression, PDF assembly, and PDF verification are performed on your Android device. To keep a draft recoverable after the app or device restarts, a photo you select with the system picker is copied once into app-private working storage while its project references it. A photo captured through the system camera is stored there for the same purpose. DocPinch does not modify or delete the original photo.

Processed pages and a temporary PDF may be stored in app-private cache while a task is running or recoverable. Clear cache removes disposable processing files. Clear project data removes the active draft, its private photo working copies, and disposable cache, but it does not delete original photos or PDFs you explicitly saved through Android's system file picker. Uninstalling the app removes its private working storage.

App backup is disabled to reduce the chance that sensitive project data or temporary documents are included in operating-system cloud backup or device transfer.

4. Android access and permissions

  • Photos: DocPinch uses Android Photo Picker. It does not request broad access to your entire photo library.
  • Camera: DocPinch can ask the system camera app to capture a page using a scoped content URI. The current design does not request the Android CAMERA runtime permission.
  • Files: Android's Storage Access Framework lets you choose where to create the PDF. DocPinch does not request All Files Access.
  • Network: Google Play Billing, Google Mobile Ads, and Google User Messaging Platform use Internet and network-state access for purchases, ads, fraud prevention, and privacy choices. These permissions are not used to upload your selected photos or generated PDFs.
  • Advertising identifier: the Google Mobile Ads SDK may access the Android advertising ID when it is available. You can reset or delete it through Android settings; Google may use other identifiers as described in its SDK disclosure.

5. Google services, advertising, and purchases

Free users may see a bottom banner. You may also choose to watch a rewarded ad; DocPinch grants 24 hours of Pro access only after the advertising SDK confirms that you earned the reward. Temporary and Lifetime Pro users do not receive new ad requests while their entitlement is active.

Where required, DocPinch asks Google User Messaging Platform to display a consent message before requesting ads. If Google requires a privacy-options entry point, it appears in DocPinch Settings so you can review or change your choice.

Google Mobile Ads may collect and share IP-address information, app interactions, diagnostic information, and device or account identifiers for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention. Its behavior can vary with your region, consent, device settings, and ad configuration. DocPinch does not pass selected photos, generated PDFs, file names, document URIs, extracted text, or document metadata to the advertising SDK.

If you view or buy Lifetime Pro, Google Play may process purchase, account, device, network, tax, fraud-prevention, and payment information under Google's own terms and privacy policy. DocPinch receives product details and purchase/entitlement state needed to unlock the feature; we do not receive your full payment-card details.

After you have successfully used the app, DocPinch may request Google Play's in-app review flow. If Google Play displays the flow, you may submit a star rating and optional review text. Google Play encrypts and processes that submission for a public Play Store review, or for private developer feedback while the app is in a closed test. You can delete a submitted review through your Google Play Store or Google Account controls.

Learn more in the Google Privacy Policy, Google Mobile Ads data disclosure, and Google Play Terms of Service.

6. Diagnostics and support

DocPinch does not automatically upload a document diagnostic log to us. The app may offer a user-initiated diagnostic report that you can review and share. Such a report is designed to contain technical information such as app/Android version, device model, processing stage, page-count bucket, image dimensions, duration, target/final byte size, and an error code.

The report is designed not to contain photo or PDF content, thumbnails, extracted text, full file paths, complete content URIs, or document file names. Do not attach sensitive documents to a public GitHub issue.

7. Retention and deletion

Because project and document data stay on your device, you control most retention directly. You can remove a recent-file entry, clear disposable cache, use Clear project data to remove the draft and its private working copies, delete saved PDFs from their destination, clear app storage, or uninstall DocPinch.

Google retains advertising, consent, and Google Play transaction information according to Google's policies, your choices, and legal obligations. Public support messages submitted to GitHub are retained according to GitHub's services and can be edited or deleted subject to GitHub account and repository controls.

8. Children

DocPinch is a general productivity utility and is not directed to children. Advertising requests are configured for a general-rated content ceiling, but the app is not enrolled as a child-directed product. We do not knowingly operate an account or document service that collects children's personal information. Parents or guardians with a concern may contact us using the method below.

9. Security and limitations

We use Android app-private storage, scoped content URIs, system pickers, read-only sharing grants where appropriate, disabled app backup, and local processing to reduce exposure. No software or storage method can guarantee absolute security. Protect access to your device and review the destination and receiving app before saving or sharing sensitive material.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when DocPinch features, permissions, dependencies, law, or Google Play requirements change. The effective date and last-updated date at the top will be revised. A material change will be reflected in the app or release notes when appropriate.

11. Contact

For a privacy or support question, open an issue in the public DocPinch privacy repository. Describe the technical problem without attaching sensitive documents, photos, PDFs, full paths, or complete content URIs.
